## Project Canvas

### Project Leverage

- Great Books course coda and bridge from canonical literature to literary journalism.
- Gay Talese’s reporting method as a practice of eliciting and preserving memory.
- “Sparks of light” as the lecture’s link between distinctive lives, writing, and shared consciousness.

### Laws / Patterns Exposed

- Memory becomes publicly transmissible when attention, encounter, and form make it vivid to others.
- The lecture contrasts withdrawal, transgression, and imaginative creation as rival paths to liberation.
- Literary journalism is presented as a way to expand a reader’s imagination through another person’s world.

### Volume Role

This chapter closes the current Great Books sequence by moving from epic and sacred poetry into literary journalism while preserving the project’s emphasis on memory and imagination.

### Strategy / Present-Day Application

Keep application at the level of public orientation: how close observation, patient interviewing, and narrative form can recover overlooked human experience.
