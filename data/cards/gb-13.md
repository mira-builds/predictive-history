# Great Books #13: Gay Talese's Sparks of Light

## Where This Sits

This is the Great Books course coda: Gay Talese and literary journalism extend the course’s concern with memory, imagination, distinctive lives, and writing beyond the canonical Homer-to-Dante sequence.

## Reading Posture

Read it as an orientation to Talese’s reporting practice and to the lecture’s “sparks of light” metaphor. Keep its interpretations of Kabbalah, sexuality, consciousness, and *Thy Neighbor’s Wife* in review.

## Historical Pressure Points

- Literary journalism as prolonged attention to another person’s memories and world.
- Talese’s mentorship and research method as a bridge between lived encounter and durable narrative.
- Rival liberation paths—withdrawal, sexual transgression, and imaginative creation.
- The claim that exceptional memories can enter shared consciousness as “sparks of light.”
